Petopia

A cosy cat care game that runs in your browser.

Raise kittens into cats, play with them in their room, teach them tricks, decorate their home and visit your friends'. Free, no download, and it works offline.

▶ Play now ⬇ Download ★ GitHub

Works on phones, tablets and computers · your save stays in your browser

What you do

🐱 Raise your cats

Feed, pat and clean up after them, and watch kittens grow into cats. Tap any need to see exactly why it's going up or down.

🎾 Play together

Chase a laser dot across your own floor, time a leap at a swinging feather, or head out fishing at the rockpool.

🎓 Teach tricks

Sit, High Five, Roll Over, Speak and Dance, and every one has a real job to do.

🛋️ Make a home

56 kinds of furniture in 21 colourways, room styles, and 30 costumes. Lamps, beds and scratching posts all make a difference.

🌦️ Live with the seasons

Aussie weather through your windows: sunbeams, rain, summer storms and the odd snowy day. Your cats feel all of it.

🌏 Visit friends

Share your room with a code, visit other players' homes, collect cat coins, and climb the leaderboards.

A look inside

Play offline

  1. Press Download and unzip it.
  2. Open Petopia.html in any modern browser.
  3. That's it. No install and no account needed.
